How Much Ah?

Ang pao amounts for weddings and Chinese New Year, plus a ledger of who gave what.

Contact

Bug, purchase problem or an amount that looks wrong: email tinhopgame@gmail.com. The quickest way is Settings → Spot a wrong amount? Email us in the app, which fills in what you picked and which version you have.

Where the amounts come from

Suggestions come from 2025–26 Malaysian wedding and Chinese New Year guides, venues' own banquet prices, and what guests say they give. They are a guide, not a rule. If your family does it differently, your family wins.

The app suggests a lucky amount near the middle of what people usually give, and skips amounts with a 4 in them. The meter (Kurang sikit, Ngam-ngam, Generous) only changes once you're clearly outside the usual range.

Your ledger

The ledger is saved on your phone only. We never see it. To move it to a new phone or keep a copy, open Settings → Back up and save the file to iCloud Drive, Files or email. On the new phone, use Settings → Restore from a backup. Deleting the app deletes the ledger, unless your phone's own backup restores it.

Lost purchases on a new phone

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
