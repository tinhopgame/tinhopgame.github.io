How Much Ah? Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · applies to the How Much Ah? app on iPhone

How Much Ah? suggests ang pao amounts and keeps a ledger of gifts you give and receive. You don't make an account, and we (the developer of How Much Ah?, "we") don't ask for your name, email or contacts.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type into the app stays on your phone: the people in your ledger, amounts, dates, venues and notes, and your answers to the questions. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless your phone's own backup restores it.

Backups and exports

If you tap Back up or Export as CSV, the app makes a file on your phone and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose where to send it, such as iCloud Drive or an email to yourself.

Emailing us

If you tap "Spot a wrong amount? Email us", your email app opens with a message that includes what you picked (for example the venue and how close you are), the amount suggested, and the app version. Nothing is sent until you press send. We only use your email to reply and to fix amounts.

Purchases (Apple App Store, RevenueCat)

Optional purchases and tips are processed by the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and let you restore them on a new phone; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for How Much Ah? and basic device information. It is not linked to your name or ledger. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • We don't run servers that store data about you.

Children

How Much Ah? is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: tinhopgame@gmail.com.
