Tinhop Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · applies to the Tinhop app on Android and iOS

Tinhop is a one-button arcade game. You don't make an account, and we (the developer of Tinhop, "we") don't ask for your name, email or contacts. This page explains the small amount of data the game and its service providers handle, and why.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, moons found, rockets and trails chosen, settings) is saved on your device only. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless your phone's own backup restores it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tinhop shows optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue after a fall) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions and diagnostic information (such as crash and performance data).

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with the AD PRIVACY CHOICES button on the How to play screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Gameplay analytics (PostHog)

To learn which levels are too hard and whether players come back, the app sends anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 14 cleared in 52 seconds" or "fell on ledge 3") to PostHog, hosted in the EU. Each install gets a random ID that is not linked to your name, account, advertising ID or contacts, and your IP address is discarded rather than stored. We use this only to improve the game. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Purchases (Google Play, Apple App Store, RevenueCat)

In-app purchases (the aim-guide pack, the no-ads bundle and cosmetic items) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and let you restore them on a new phone; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for Tinhop and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sharing clips

If you tap SHARE CLIP, the game makes a short GIF of your last few seconds on your phone and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose where to send it. Saving to Photos asks for your permission first.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We don't run our own servers that store data about you, and analytics can't identify you.

Your choices

  • On iPhone, you can say no when the app asks to track you, or turn tracking off for all apps in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads still appear, but they aren't personalised.
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. To ask about the anonymous gameplay data, email us with the date you installed the game.

Where data is processed

Our service providers may process data outside your country. Google and RevenueCat process data mainly in the United States; PostHog stores gameplay events in the European Union. Each provider protects data under its own privacy terms.

Children

Tinhop is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it. Ads in Tinhop are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Security and retention

Data sent to our providers is encrypted in transit. Providers keep data according to their own policies linked above. Because we hold no data about you ourselves, there is nothing on our side to delete; uninstalling the app removes your local progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
